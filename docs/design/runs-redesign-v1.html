Runs NIGHT BUILD · v1 PROPOSAL

Stop looking like Settings. Start looking like 11pm on the Second Link.

A redesign proposal for Runs — six screens, a new token system, and one structural change to the navigation. Nothing here breaches runs-v1-spec.md: no speed, no leader, no chat, no new backend fields. Review it, then I build it in app/src/ui/.

App theme
Both ship. Night is the default — you drive at night.
The read

Three problems, only one of them is paint

No identity

Every surface is PlatformColor("systemGroupedBackground") plus an orange tint. The craft is real — you documented WCAG ratios in theme.ts — but strip the tint and it's a utilities app.

The map is buried

Your spec says "glanceable in under a second." LiveMapScreen is two taps deep behind RunDetailScreen. That's the fix that matters most, and it isn't a colour.

Nothing says cars

A live run with six cars on the road and a meet from three weeks ago render as the same ListRow. carName is a grey subtitle. Hierarchy is doing no work.

Foundations

Sodium, asphalt, road markings

Palette pulled from what a night drive actually looks like — sodium-vapour street light on wet tarmac, signage green, headlight white. Your existing brand orange survives, brightened for a dark ground. Contrast ratios computed against the surface each colour sits on, the same way theme.ts already documents them.

The motif

The dot trail

It's already in your logo, and ArrivalDots already draws it. Promote it to the whole system: one dot per person, filled when they're at the meetup. It's the convoy, strung out along the road.

The structural change

The live dock

This is the one change that isn't paint, and the one I'd fight for. Today, getting to the map while driving is: open app → find the run → tap it → wait for RunDetail to load → tap "Open Live Map". Five steps, two network round-trips, while moving.

Today
  1. Open app — RunListScreen
  2. Find the active run in a list of three sections
  3. Tap it — navigate, fetch, render RunDetailScreen
  4. Scroll past hero, route group, RSVP group
  5. Tap "Open Live Map" — navigate again

Your spec, R5: "glanceable in under a second."

Proposed
  1. Open app — the dock is already there, on every screen
  2. Tap Map

A persistent bar above the safe area whenever a run you've joined is active. It shows arrival count, run name, and the live "car ahead" distance — so most of the time you don't even need to open the map. It disappears the moment the run ends, which is also a privacy signal: dock visible = you are sharing.

How it's built

No navigator change. A <LiveDock /> rendered once in App.tsx beneath <Stack.Navigator>, reading activeSessionRunId() and the latest snapshot from liveSession.ts — both of which are already module-level singletons with a subscribe API. It hides itself on LiveMap and on modal presentations. Screens gain bottom padding when it's visible.

Before I write code

Five things only you can decide

Dark-only, or both themes?

I've drawn both. Night-only is the stronger design and halves the work. But your app.json says "userInterfaceStyle": "automatic", and half your group will be on a bright phone at a 7am breakfast run.

My call: ship both. Default to Night regardless of system setting, with a switch in You. A convoy app that flashes white at 11pm is a real problem.

Plate numbers as a real field?

Everything above uses your existing free-text carName, typeset as a plate. A genuine plate field (JQK 8899) would make the board scannable at a physical meetup — you find the car before you find the face.

My call: not in v1. It's a schema change, a migration, and a privacy question for a 50-person group. Park it.

Does the live dock survive contact?

It's the change with the most upside and the most risk — it eats ~64px on every screen and it's the one thing I can't validate without you holding the phone in a car.

My call: build it, put it in front of three people on a real run, keep or kill it after. Don't debate it in a doc.

Map strip on the home screen?

The hero card's mini-map is the single most expensive element here: a MapLibre instance or snapshot on the list screen, plus tile fetches. It's also what makes the screen feel like a car app.

My call: ship v1 with a flat ground and the dot trail. Add the map strip only if the list screen stays fast.

All at once, or theme first?

Big-bang reskin, or land theme.ts + components.tsx first and migrate screens one PR at a time?

My call: tokens first, then Runs, then Run detail, then the rest. Your tests in src/lib/__tests__ don't touch UI, so nothing protects you from a big-bang regression.
